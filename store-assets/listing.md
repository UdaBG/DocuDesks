**Short description**
Sign and edit PDFs fully offline. Bulk signing, OCR, no ads — free forever.

**Full description**
DocuDesk signs and edits PDF documents entirely on your phone. No account, no upload, no internet — the app doesn't even request the internet permission. Your documents never leave your device.

SIGN ONE, SIGN THEM ALL
• Draw, photograph, or type your signature and save it for reuse
• Load a stack of PDFs and sign every one in the same spot with one tap
• Smart detect finds the signature line in each document automatically
• Place, resize, and rotate multiple signatures per document

A COMPLETE PDF EDITOR

• Works on scans too, with built-in offline text recognition (OCR)
• Vertical or rotated labels stay rotated when you retype them
• Text, pen, highlighter, shapes, arrows, whiteout, and watermarks — all rotatable
• Add, duplicate, reorder, or delete pages; merge documents
• Unlock password-protected PDFs on the spot (your original file is never changed)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline — nothing is ever uploaded
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Free forever, with no in-app purchases

Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Swedish, and Sinhala.
